About This Project

This project uses the DOHMH New York City Restaurant Inspection Results dataset from NYC Open Data. The dataset includes information about restaurant inspections, boroughs, cuisine types, inspection grades, scores, violations, and inspection dates.

Agency Background

The data is provided by the New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, also called DOHMH. DOHMH helps protect public health in New York City, including inspecting restaurants and food service establishments.

Why This Dataset Matters

Restaurant inspection data is important because it helps people understand food safety conditions in different parts of New York City. This website makes the data easier to view, filter, and analyze.

Website Sections

The Filter Data page lets users view restaurant inspection records as cards and filter them by borough, grade, and restaurant name. The Analysis page uses charts to compare inspection results by borough and grade.
